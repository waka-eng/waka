WWAKA · Wow Moment Engine

Product thesis

The wow is the relationship, not the classification.

Every new inspiration should trigger discovery. But the system should use embeddings and heuristics to search cheaply, then ask the LLM only to judge a small candidate set.

Goal: make WAKA feel like it found meaning in the user's archive — recurring motifs, hidden bridges, real tensions, and surprising outliers.

Example reveal

“This unexpectedly rhymes with your earlier notes on ritual spaces.”

InputUser saves a messy thought, link, or image.

JudgeLLM confirms whether the connection is defensible, not forced.

What contributes to a wow moment?

Measure the shape around the new inspiration.

Semantic resonance

How close is it to prior ideas?

Use cosine similarity against stored inspiration embeddings. Strong neighbors become motif or recurrence candidates.

Time spread

Did the idea come back after time?

A pattern spanning weeks or months feels more revealing than several saves from the same afternoon.

Cluster diversity

Does it connect different zones?

If related inspirations come from multiple clusters, the new item may be a bridge or convergence point.

Richness

Is the input substantial enough?

Length, links, attachments, questions, and reflection depth prevent thin notes from generating noisy wow moments.

Salience

Is the older item important?

Reflections, revisits, prior wow involvement, age, and content richness make a distant candidate worth comparing.

Contrast

Is there a meaningful opposition?

Tension often appears when two related ideas pull in different directions: freedom vs control, simple vs dense.

Archive distance

Is this outside the user's usual map?

Low max similarity plus low centroid similarity suggests a possible outlier or new frontier.

Guardrail

Run discovery every time. Show wow only when earned.

Not every save deserves a reveal. Detection should be constant; visible moments should be selective.

Incremental algorithm

No all-history LLM scan on insertion.

Use cheap vector retrieval and scoring first. Spend LLM tokens only after candidates pass thresholds.

Save inspiration

Embedding search finds “what might matter.” Heuristics rank “what is probably interesting.” The LLM decides “is this actually a wow moment, and how should we say it?”

Kind taxonomy

Five MVP wow kinds, including surprise and tension.

Recurring

Motif

The user keeps returning to the same idea, aesthetic, question, or desire.

Signals: high avgTop5 similarity · related count ≥ 3 · time spread
Connected

Bridge

The new item connects multiple known clusters or themes in the archive.

Signals: cluster diversity · multi-cluster similarity · related density
Surprise

Surprise bridge

Surface domains are different, but the deeper structure, pattern, or question rhymes.

Signals: mid-distance similarity · high salience · different clusters · LLM-confirmed analogy
Friction

Tension

Two related inspirations pull in opposite directions: values, stance, assumptions, or desires conflict.

Signals: medium-high similarity · contrast hints · tension cluster · LLM-confirmed opposition
Frontier

Outlier

A rich new inspiration sits outside the user's current archive and may start a new thread.

Signals: low max similarity · low centroid similarity · high richness · enough archive history

Important

Embeddings do not output kind.

Embedding = coordinates. Heuristics = measurements over those coordinates. Kind = interpretation of the pattern. LLM = final semantic judge for hard cases.

Decision gates

Discovery always runs. Visible wow moments must earn trust.

Cheap gates

  • Archive has enough history
  • Input passes richness minimum
  • Candidate score clears threshold
  • Limit to top one or two candidate kinds

LLM gates

  • Reject generic or forced connections
  • Require one-sentence defensible explanation
  • For tension: same topic, opposing values
  • For surprise: different domains, shared structure

Saved record

  • kind
  • score
  • title
  • short explanation
  • related inspiration IDs
  • reflection question
Recommended MVP: motif, bridge, surprise_bridge, tension, and outlier. Require LLM confirmation for tension and surprise_bridge; use careful language for outliers.
