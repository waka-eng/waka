# Privacy Policy

WAKA operates a personal memory service. This policy explains how we handle information when you create an account, save or share something, or use WAKA’s AI features. For privacy questions or requests, email [team@waka.im](mailto:team@waka.im).

## Information we handle

- Account and profile information. This includes your email address, profile details you choose to provide, and authentication information. If you sign in with Google, we receive information such as your Google account identifier and verified email address.
- Things you save and write. This includes notes, links, text retrieved from links you save, photos, files, recordings, chats, and feedback. We generate related material such as transcripts, summaries, and numerical representations that help WAKA find relevant material.
- Technical and security information. This includes session information, signup and sign-in attempts, and operational information needed to keep WAKA reliable and protect accounts.

## How WAKA uses information

We use information to provide the features you use, including sign-in, storage, search, conversations, and suggestions. We also use it to answer support requests, protect the service, diagnose problems, enforce usage limits, and meet legal obligations.

Sharing controls determine what other WAKA users can see. If you have a public profile, it can show your chosen profile details and, if separately enabled, selected material WAKA prepared from your memory. Group sharing lets other members see the statements you share. Your supporting notes stay private from the group. People may keep copies of what you publish or share.

## Service providers and processing outside your country

- Amazon Web Services and Neon help us run the service and store data. Google helps authenticate people who choose Google sign-in.
- OpenAI, Google Gemini, and OpenRouter help us provide AI features. OpenRouter sends requests to the model provider serving the request. Depending on the feature, a request can include portions of notes, chat messages, relevant saved material, search text, images, or recordings. For example, processing a recording can send audio to a provider, while AI-powered search can send text to create or look up numerical representations.
- We use PostHog on the production website for limited events such as which screen opened and categories of browser errors. WAKA uses a random identifier kept in browser memory and disables session recording.

Providers may process information in the United States and other countries. Their handling of request data, including retention for safety and abuse monitoring, depends on the service, provider, and applicable settings. Data can also be disclosed where required by law or to protect people, accounts, and the service.

## Cookies and similar technology

WAKA uses cookies and similar storage to keep you signed in, complete authentication, and remember information needed for the website to work. You can manage cookies in your browser. Disabling cookies needed for authentication can prevent sign-in.

## Retention and deletion

We keep account and memory information while needed to provide your account and the features you use. Deleted saves are normally available to restore for 30 days. Expired items are removed through cleanup. Account deletion is a separate action you can take in Settings.

Account deletion removes your account, its private memory, and attachments associated with it from the active service. Settled group records can remain with your attribution removed. Copies you’ve shared with others or sent to a connected app can also remain with those recipients. Backup copies, where maintained, and provider logs follow separate retention schedules. Limited signup, security, and legal records can be kept separately from your memory.

## Your choices and privacy rights

You can edit or delete saved items, revoke API keys, and delete your account. A signed-in human account can request a JSON export of a profile it owns. It includes saved material and attachment information. For file copies, account information beyond that export, or help with export or public sharing, contact us.

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to request access, correction, deletion, or limits on how we process your information. Email [team@waka.im](mailto:team@waka.im). We may request information to verify your account before giving access or making changes. If you believe we mishandled your information, you may contact the privacy authority that serves your location.

## Eligibility and updates

WAKA is intended for adults. Contact us if you believe someone under 18 provided personal information through WAKA. We’ll update the effective date if this policy changes and provide any notice required by law.
